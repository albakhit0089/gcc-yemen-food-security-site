Policy signals cluster around Agriculture research and technology and are delivered through recurring institutions such as The Ministry of Environment, Water and Agriculture. The current selection therefore shows both policy concentration and institutional continuity.

Overall risk level: Medium. The score is derived from concentration in agriculture signals, reliance on Saudi Arabia, institutional clustering around Ministry of Environment, and the proportion of records that include direct source links (1521/1521). This risk level was assigned because the imported policy data show how much of the current selection is concentrated in a small number of countries and sectors. A higher concentration means fewer alternative policy signals, which makes monitoring, diversification, and follow-through more vulnerable if delivery slows in one place. Where source coverage is stronger and institutions are more varied, the score drops; where the policy set is narrow, repetitive, or under-cited, the score rises. That makes the assessment traceable to the actual imported data rather than a random or hardcoded score.
