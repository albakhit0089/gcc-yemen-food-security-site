The Ministry of Environment, Water and Agriculture highlighted tree planting as a natural measure to reduce summer temperatures

Saudi Arabia
The Ministry of Environment, Water and Agriculture
Climate change mitigation and adaptation measures
Source available

The Ministry of Environment, Water and Agriculture highlighted tree planting as a natural measure to reduce summer temperatures, noting that trees lower heat through shading, reduced heat absorption, and transpiration, while each tree can absorb an average of 150 kilograms of carbon dioxide annually. The ministry stated that expanding green spaces improves air quality, reduces noise, supports rainwater management, and provides habitats for wildlife and pollinators. It recommended planting native tree species adapted to local climatic conditions and advised irrigation during early morning or evening using drip irrigation systems, together with organic mulch to conserve soil moisture and regulate soil temperature.

Yemen

Source available

The Agriculture Sector in Dhamar Governorate conducted a field training workshop for farmers on the safe use of agricultural pesticides. The programme provided guidance on pesticide handling, personal protective equipment, integrated pest management, crop rotation, pest identification, correct application rates, pre-harvest safety intervals, first aid for pesticide poisoning, and the proper disposal of pesticide containers. Officials also warned against the use of banned or smuggled pesticides and highlighted unsafe practices observed among farmers, while distributing awareness materials at the end of the workshop.

The partnership focuses on renewable energy development and expanding access to clean energy solutions in Yemen and the wider Middle East.

Researchers at King Abdullah University of Science and Technology (KAUST) developed a new genome engineering method that enables the precise insertion of large genetic sequences into specific locations within plant genomes without creating DNA breaks. The method was successfully demonstrated in tobacco and rice and expands the capabilities of plant biotechnology for developing crops with complex genetic traits, including improved tolerance to heat, drought, and disease, as well as supporting plant-based biomanufacturing.

The Saudi Ports Authority (Mawani) signed seven contracts worth SAR1 billion to expand logistics centres at Jeddah Islamic Port and the Al Khumra logistics zone. The projects will develop nearly 384,000 square metres of logistics infrastructure for storage, consolidation, and re-export, supported by digital technologies and enhanced transport connectivity. The expansion includes new container yards in partnership with Maersk and is expected to improve supply chain efficiency, increase trade capacity, create more than 5,000 jobs, and strengthen Saudi Arabia’s position as a regional and global logistics hub under Saudi Vision 2030 and the National Transport and Logistics Strategy.
