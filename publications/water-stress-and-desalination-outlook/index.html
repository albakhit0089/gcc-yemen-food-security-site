Water Stress & Desalination Outlook in GCC Countries

A technical report on water stress drivers, desalination capacity, irrigation efficiency, and groundwater trends across the region.

Publication Summary

Executive Summary

Water stress remains high across GCC countries, with desalination providing critical supply buffering. The main long-term opportunity is improving water productivity across agriculture and urban demand.

Objectives

  • •Review supply and demand dynamics driving water stress.
  • •Assess desalination, irrigation, and groundwater metrics.
  • •Identify priority actions for water security.

Key Findings

  • •Desalination provides system stability but increases energy exposure.
  • •Irrigation modernization improves water productivity when paired with governance.
  • •Groundwater depletion remains a key sustainability risk.

Recommendations

  • •Scale efficiency incentives and irrigation modernization programs.
  • •Improve groundwater monitoring and extraction controls.
  • •Integrate climate risk planning into water supply investment decisions.
